Need to know?

Digital service FAQs

Do I need a library membership?

Many digital resources are intended for members. Visit the Membership page for information about joining.

Can I use these services on my phone?

Yes. The pages and resources are designed to work across modern phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

What if I cannot access a resource?

Check that your membership details are correct and contact the library team for support.
